# End-of-Year Banquet Plan — Student Group, Berkeley CA (~30 guests, $2,500 target / $3,000 cap)

## TL;DR

- **Book the Kinzie Room at the David Brower Center** (2150 Allston Way, Downtown Berkeley; [events@browercenter.org][ref-01] / 510-809-0900) for a weekday spring evening — the College/University rate is $515 for a 4-hour evening block plus a $350 AV package — and cater a **Mediterranean buffet from La Méditerranée** (2936 College Ave, Berkeley; 510-540-7773) whose naturally egg-, nut-, peanut-, and shellfish-free mains (chicken kebab, lamb meatballs, falafel, hummus/baba ghanoush, salads, rice pilaf) keep the whole buffet safe by default, with dairy (feta, yogurt sauce) served on the side.
- **The full plan totals about $2,366 including a 10% contingency** — roughly $134 under the $2,500 target and $634 under the $3,000 cap — leaving room to upgrade to full-service catering or the projector-equipped Tamalpais Room and still stay under the cap. No alcohol is included (assumption noted below).
- **The single most important allergy move is a default-safe buffet, not special plates:** because cross-contact (shared serving spoons, adjacent pans, hidden dairy/egg in sauces) is the real risk with 2 egg, 2 peanut, 1 dairy+tree-nut+shellfish, and 1 vegetarian guest present at once, a menu where every main is inherently free of those allergens protects everyone; dairy goes in separate labeled bowls with dedicated utensils.

## Key Findings

- **Best venue: David Brower Center, Kinzie Room.** LEED-platinum, stylish downtown space [David Brower Center][ref-02] (not a bare classroom), one block from Downtown Berkeley BART. [browercenter][ref-03] [David Brower Center][ref-03] Kinzie is 936 sq ft (26' × 36'), seats 56 banquet / 80 reception / 75 theater — right-sized for 30. College/University tier: **Quarter Day $515 / Half Day $845 / Full Day $1,195**; [browercenter][ref-04] the evening quarter-day block is "up to 4 hours of occupancy between... 4 pm and midnight" (fits a 3-hour banquet plus setup/breakdown). AV package **$350**. A 50% deposit is due at signing with the balance one month prior.
- **Projector caveat — resolve before signing:** Kinzie's standard $350 AV package explicitly lists a **50″ HDMI monitor** (plus PA system, laptop, up to 2 wireless mics, and 3 hours of AV staff), *not* a projector. The Brower Center's own materials state projection screens exist "in all event spaces" and that Kinzie has "full audio/visual capabilities" including a projection system, but the default package provides a monitor. A 50″ monitor may be too small for 30 guests to read slides from the back, so **confirm a projector + screen in writing.** If a dedicated projection screen is essential, the Tamalpais Room (explicitly configured for "seated screening") [The Vendry][ref-05] is the upgrade.
- **Best caterer: La Méditerranée (Berkeley).** Chef Levon Der Bedrossian opened the Berkeley location with his cousin Garbis Baghdassarian in 1982 (his first San Francisco/Fillmore location opened in 1979). Mediterranean/Middle Eastern is the strongest cuisine match for this allergy profile: mains are naturally egg-free, peanut-free, tree-nut-free, and shellfish-free, and dairy is easy to isolate. Published catering trays give concrete pricing (e.g., chicken-kebab-over-rice-pilaf tray "$138.00 — Serves 10 people," including organic mixed-green salad, hummus, pita, and compostable cutlery). **Note:** La Méditerranée is not on the Brower Center's preferred-caterer list, so it requires the Center's prior approval and may incur an outside-caterer fee — budget for this or use the onsite partner (Gather) instead.
- **Sales tax on catering:** Prepared/hot food is taxable. Berkeley's countywide base is 10.25%, but current 2026 rate tables (SalesTaxHandbook, April 2026) list **10.75% for ZIP codes 94704 and 94705** — the venue and caterer ZIPs — reflecting a recent city district-tax increase; some sources (e.g., the UC Berkeley Controller) still cite 10.25%. This plan budgets at the higher 10.75% to be safe; **confirm the exact rate on your final invoice.**
- **Outside catering is the key contractual variable at Brower:** "Caterers not on our list are subject to our prior approval and additional fees may apply." [browercenter][ref-04] Gather, located inside the Brower Center, [gatherberkeley][ref-06] is the frictionless onsite option with no outside-caterer fee.

### Allergy & Cross-Contact Protocol (put in writing with the caterer)

Confirm before signing: dedicated prep surfaces and cutting boards for allergen-free items; **separate serving utensils for every dish**; ingredient/allergen labels at the buffet; **no shared fryer** (critical for falafel vs. any battered/fried item); no sesame or nut oils in cooking; and verification that sauces/marinades contain **no hidden dairy or egg** (mayo, aioli, Caesar dressing, butter in rice pilaf, yogurt in marinades, fish sauce/Worcestershire). Ask that dairy (feta, yogurt sauce, cheese) be plated separately, invite allergic guests through the buffet first, and request an advance ingredient list to share with the affected guests. Falafel binder should be confirmed egg-free (traditional falafel is chickpea-only); meatball binder should be confirmed egg/breadcrumb status or clearly labeled. Industry guidance is explicit that "cross-contact refers to allergen transfer... allergen proteins cannot be destroyed by cooking," so trace exposure (e.g., peanut oil, a shared spoon) is a genuine ER risk, not a preference issue — which is exactly why the default-safe buffet design matters more than special plates.

## Recommendations

1. 1. **Now (8–10 weeks out):** Submit the Brower Center inquiry form for the Kinzie Room on a weekday spring evening; confirm the projector/screen and lock the $350 AV package in writing. Pay the 50% deposit. Simultaneously call La Méditerranée to confirm catering availability, the exact delivery fee, lead time, and whether full-service staffing is available — and ask Brower to approve La Méditerranée as an off-list caterer (get any fee in writing).
2. 2. **6–8 weeks out:** Finalize the menu above; send the caterer the full dietary list (2 egg, 2 peanut, 1 dairy+tree-nut+shellfish, 1 vegetarian) and the written cross-contact protocol. If Brower's outside-caterer fee is high, switch to Gather.
3. 3. **4 weeks out:** Collect dietary info from every attendee via a form; pay the venue balance (due one month prior).
4. 4. **2 weeks / 7 business days out:** Confirm the Banquet Event Order and final guaranteed headcount; order allergen labels; finalize décor and printing.
5. 5. **1 week out:** Walkthrough and AV/slideshow test in the room; print programs and awards.
6. 6. **Day before:** Reconfirm delivery time and the allergy protocol with the caterer.

- **Benchmarks that change the plan:** If the projector cannot be confirmed for Kinzie, switch to the Tamalpais Room (+$75). If Brower's outside-caterer fee exceeds ~$500, switch to Gather onsite. If headcount rises above ~40, move to the Tamalpais Room and add one more chicken tray (+$138).

### Questions to Ask Before Signing

- **Venue:** Is a projector + screen (not just the 50″ monitor) available in Kinzie, and is it in the $350 package? Are security/janitorial staff required for a weekday evening (and their cost)? Is our off-list caterer approved, and is there a fee? Are linens available or must we rent them? What is the exact load-in/out window?
- **Caterer:** Can you make the entire hot buffet egg-, peanut-, tree-nut-, and shellfish-free with dairy served separately? Do you use dedicated boards/utensils and avoid a shared fryer? What's in the rice pilaf, marinades, and dressings (any butter, yogurt, mayo, egg)? What's the delivery fee, minimum order, lead time, and final-count deadline? Is full-service staffing available and at what cost? Can you provide an advance ingredient list and allergen labels?

## Caveats

- **Prices should be reconfirmed by phone/email.** Venue rates, the AV package contents (monitor vs. projector), and the Brower fee structure are from current published sources; the sales-tax rate differs by source (10.25% vs. 10.75% for 94704/94705) and should be verified on the final invoice; caterer delivery fees, full-service staffing costs, exact lead times, and any Brower outside-caterer fee were not fully published online and are realistic estimates flagged "confirm."
- **The 50″ monitor vs. projector distinction is the biggest open item** — resolve it before signing, or default to the Tamalpais Room.
- **La Méditerranée requires Brower approval as an off-list caterer;** if that adds meaningful cost or is declined, Gather (onsite) is the clean fallback with no outside-caterer fee.
- **Allergy safety depends on the caterer's written commitment,** not just the menu; do not proceed without confirming the cross-contact protocol. Baklava and cheese (nuts/dairy) must be physically separated from the shared dessert.
- **Alcohol is excluded;** adding it requires a licensed provider and would likely push the event over the target and near the cap.
