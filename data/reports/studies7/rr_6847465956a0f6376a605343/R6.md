# Student Group End-of-Year Banquet Plan: Berkeley, CA

**Abstract**

This report presents a complete, budget-compliant plan for a 30-person student group end-of-year banquet near Berkeley, California. The recommended venue is the **Tamalpais Room at the David Brower Center** (2150 Allston Way, Berkeley), which offers a built-in projector, AV system, and versatile seating for up to 80 banquet guests—well above the 30-person requirement. The recommended caterer is **The Mixing Bowl Catering**, a Brower Center–approved partner and UC Berkeley Eat Well Program participant, whose **Hot Entrée Option 10** menu (\$28.75/person) provides a BBQ chicken entrée, a vegan jackfruit entrée, and two vegan sides—all free of eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, and shellfish. The total estimated cost of approximately **\$2,100–\$2,340** falls comfortably within the \$2,500 target budget and well under the \$3,000 hard cap, with no transportation costs included.

## Venue Recommendation: David Brower Center — Tamalpais Room

### Location and Accessibility

The David Brower Center is located at **2150 Allston Way, Suite 100, Berkeley, CA 94704**, in the heart of Downtown Berkeley—just one block from the UC Berkeley campus and one block from the Downtown Berkeley BART station (1)(2). The building is a LEED Platinum–certified nonprofit center designed as a hub for environmental and community events. For a UC Berkeley–affiliated student group, this location is essentially on campus, making it extremely convenient for attendees walking from campus or taking public transit.

Key location advantages:

- **Walking distance from campus**: Approximately 1–2 blocks from the central UC Berkeley campus
- **Public transit**: One block from Downtown Berkeley BART; multiple AC Transit bus lines stop nearby
- **Parking**: The City of Berkeley's Oxford Garage is located directly underneath the Brower Center (enter at 2161 Kittredge), offering hourly parking (1)
- **ADA accessibility**: The second-floor Tamalpais Room is accessible via an ADA-compliant elevator; the building also offers assisted-listening devices for events in the Tamalpais Room (1)

### Room Specifications and Capacity

The Tamalpais Room is the Brower Center's most versatile event space, located on the second floor (1):

| Specification | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| Room dimensions | 35' × 45' |
| Square footage | 1,575 sq. ft. |
| Standing reception capacity | 120 |
| Theater seating capacity | 120 |
| Banquet seating capacity | 80 |
| Classroom seating capacity | 72 |

| U-shape capacity | 39 |

For a 30-person banquet, the Tamalpais Room offers ample space—accommodating up to 80 in banquet configuration, meaning the group will have a comfortable, spacious layout rather than a cramped arrangement. The room features tackboard walls, full audio/visual capabilities with high-definition digital video, and sound reinforcement by Meyer Sound (1).

### Audio/Visual Equipment

This is a critical requirement for the student group, which needs a projector for slides. The Tamalpais Room's AV system fully meets this need (1):

**AV Package (Standard) — \$425**, which includes:
- PA system
- **Projector** (the primary requirement)
- Laptop
- Up to 4 wireless handheld microphones and cables
- 3 hours of AV staff time (for setup, support, and breakdown)

Additional AV staff time is available at \$60/hour after the first 3 hours if needed. A **Videoconferencing Hardware Upgrade** is also available for \$125 (not required for this event but noted for future reference if hybrid attendance is desired) (1).

The AV package is mandatory if the group wishes to use the projector and sound system, and it is staffed by a house technician who handles setup and breakdown. This means the student group does not need to bring or operate any of its own AV equipment.

### Rental Rates and Fee Structure

The Brower Center offers tiered pricing based on the renter category. A student group affiliated with UC Berkeley should qualify for the **"Colleges & Universities" rate** (1):

| Rate Category | Quarter Day | Half Day | Full Day |

**Time definitions** (1):
- **Quarter Day**: Up to 4 hours of occupancy (7 am–3 pm or 4 pm–midnight)
- **Half Day**: Up to 8 hours of occupancy (7 am–3 pm or 4 pm–midnight)
- **Full Day**: Up to 12 hours of occupancy (7 am–midnight)

**Recommendation: Quarter Day rate (\$590)**. A 4-hour window is sufficient for a typical end-of-year banquet (e.g., 5:00 pm–9:00 pm, falling within the 4 pm–midnight Quarter Day window). This allows time for setup (30–45 min), the banquet itself with slides and program (2–2.5 hours), and cleanup (30–45 min).

**Potential additional fees** (1):
- **Weekend facility fee**: \$100 per floor for space preparation and cleanup (applies to weekend bookings only). The Tamalpais Room is on the second floor, so this would be a flat \$100 if the event is on a Saturday or Sunday.

> **Note on rate qualification**: The "Colleges & Universities" rate is listed as a distinct category from "Nonprofit." A UC Berkeley student group should contact the Brower Center Events Office (events@browercenter.org or 510-809-0900) to confirm which rate applies and whether the student group qualifies for the nonprofit rate instead, which would save an additional \$10.

This labeling system makes it straightforward to verify that selected items accommodate all four dietary restrictions in the group.

### Selected Menu: Hot Entrée Option 10

**Price: \$28.75/person** (minimum 25 people) (3)

The complete menu for Option 10 is:

| Item | Dietary Labels | Description |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **BBQ Chicken with Chipotle Honey Mustard Glaze** | GF | Grilled chicken with a smoky-sweet chipotle honey mustard glaze |
| **Memphis BBQ Jackfruit** | VG, GF | Plant-based jackfruit with tangy chipotle molasses sauce and smoked paprika—a vegan BBQ entrée |
| **Roasted Herbed Potato Salad** | VG, GF | Mustard frisée, green onions, celery, and mustard vinaigrette |
| **Garden Salad** | VG, GF | Mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, shredded carrots, cucumbers, and house vinaigrette |

**Why this menu was selected:**

1. **Complete dietary restriction coverage**: Every item in Option 10 is free of eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, and shellfish—verified by the absence of "nuts" and "dairy" allergen icons on all four items, and confirmed by the VG (vegan) and GF (gluten-free) labels on three of the four items.

2. **Built-in vegetarian/vegan option**: The Memphis BBQ Jackfruit (VG, GF) serves as a substantial, flavorful plant-based main course that satisfies the vegetarian attendee and all allergen-restricted attendees simultaneously. Jackfruit, when prepared with BBQ sauce, has a pulled-pork-like texture that is widely appreciated even by non-vegetarians.

3. **Buffet-style service**: Hot Entrée packages are served buffet-style, allowing each attendee to choose between the chicken and jackfruit entrée. This is ideal for a mixed-dietary group—everyone can eat from the same spread without needing individually plated special meals.

4. **No hidden allergens**: Unlike several other Hot Entrée Options (e.g., Option 3 with slivered almonds, Option 8 with shrimp paella, Option 9 with spicy peanut dressing), Option 10 contains no nuts, shellfish, or peanut-derived ingredients in any of its components.

5. **Reasonable price**: At \$28.75/person, the total food cost for 30 people is \$862.50—well within the catering budget even after adding tax, delivery, and place settings.

## Allergen Safety Protocols and Recommendations

Given the severity and multiplicity of dietary restrictions in this group, the following protocols are strongly recommended:

### 1. Pre-Order Allergen Verification

Contact The Mixing Bowl Catering at least **2 weeks** before the event to:
- Request a **written allergen confirmation** for all four items in Hot Entrée Option 10, specifically verifying the absence of: eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, dairy (including whey, casein, butter, cream), and shellfish
- Ask about **cross-contamination prevention**: Does the kitchen use shared equipment for nut-containing and nut-free dishes? Are separate prep areas used for allergen-free orders?
- Confirm the **BBQ Chicken** is prepared without any dairy-based ingredients in the glaze or marinade (honey mustard glazes sometimes contain butter or cream)
- Request that the caterer **label each dish** on the buffet with ingredient/allergen cards

### 2. Buffet Labeling and Layout

- Request that The Mixing Bowl provides **ingredient cards** for each dish on the buffet table
- Arrange the buffet so that the vegan items (Memphis BBQ Jackfruit, Potato Salad, Garden Salad) are placed **first** in the serving line, followed by the BBQ Chicken—this reduces the risk of cross-contamination from serving utensils touching multiple dishes
- Provide **separate serving utensils** for each dish (the compostable place setting includes individual plates, but serving utensils should be distinct per dish)

### 3. Communication with Attendees

- Include dietary information in the event invitation so attendees know what will be served
- Designate one student group member as the **dietary restriction point person** who can answer questions and direct attendees with concerns to the labeled dishes
- For the attendee with the combined dairy + nuts + shellfish allergy, confirm directly that they are comfortable with the menu and cross-contamination risk level

### 4. Emergency Preparedness

- Ask attendees with severe allergies to carry their **EpiPens** to the event
- Ensure at least one student group leader knows the location of the nearest emergency room (Alta Bates Summit Medical Center is approximately 1.5 miles from the Brower Center)
- Save the caterer's phone number (510-655-5630) in case any ingredient questions arise during the event

### Critical Risk: Weekend Security Staffing

The most significant budget risk is the potential requirement for a security guard at weekend evening events, at the discretion of the Brower Center Events Director (\$700 flat fee for a 5-hour minimum). If this applies:

- This is still **under the \$3,000 hard cap** but exceeds the \$2,500 target by ~\$382

A Friday evening event (4 pm–midnight Quarter Day window) would avoid weekend fees entirely while still being convenient for students.
