# Comprehensive Event Operational Plan and Financial Analysis for UC Berkeley Student Banquet

## Executive Summary and Event Parameters

Organizing an end-of-year banquet for an academic student group requires a carefully structured operational plan that balances financial parameters, venue logistics, audiovisual technology, and rigorous dietary safety. This operational plan establishes a complete strategy for hosting a 30-person student banquet near the University of California, Berkeley campus[[1]](https://www.mihiphotobooth.com/post/best-events-venue-2026-berkeley-ca/). The project is governed by a baseline target budget of $2,500.00 and a strict hard price ceiling of $3,000.00, explicitly excluding transportation expenditures.

The primary event objectives dictate securing a high-quality venue equipped with integrated audiovisual (AV) capabilities—specifically a dedicated projector and screen for slide presentations[[2]](https://www.downtownberkeley.com/venues/). Furthermore, the event requires custom catering capable of accommodating four distinct, high-risk dietary profiles across the 30 attendees: two individuals with egg allergies, two with peanut allergies, one with a complex combination allergy to dairy, tree nuts, peanuts, and shellfish, and one vegetarian attendee[[3]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/cal-spirit/alumni-house-rental/). Through rigorous comparative venue analysis and precise menu engineering, this plan identifies the David Brower Center and its in-house culinary partner, Gather Catering, as the optimal execution pairing[[1]](https://www.mihiphotobooth.com/post/best-events-venue-2026-berkeley-ca/).

### Primary Venue Choice: David Brower Center (Tamalpais Room)

The primary venue recommendation is the **Tamalpais Room at the David Brower Center**, situated at 2150 Allston Way in Downtown Berkeley[[1]](https://www.mihiphotobooth.com/post/best-events-venue-2026-berkeley-ca/). Located one block from the UC Berkeley campus edge and the Downtown Berkeley BART station, this LEED Platinum-certified facility offers high accessibility and environmentally sustainable building operations[[1]](https://www.mihiphotobooth.com/post/best-events-venue-2026-berkeley-ca/). The center utilizes non-toxic materials throughout its event spaces and features integrated Meyer Sound acoustic engineering[[2]](https://www.downtownberkeley.com/venues/).

The Tamalpais Room provides a flexible layout suitable for group banquets up to 120 guests[[5]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/event-faq/). Qualifying under the **Colleges & Universities** rental rate structure, a Quarter Day occupancy block (granting up to 4 hours of facility access between 7:00 AM–3:00 PM or 4:00 PM–Midnight) incurs a base rental charge of $590.00[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/). The room's Standard Audio/Visual Package costs $425.00 and directly fulfills the presentation requirements[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/). This package includes a high-definition projector, projection screen, laptop, PA system, up to four wireless handheld microphones, and three hours of dedicated house AV technician staffing to manage setup and live presentation controls[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/). The venue supplies 60-inch round banquet tables, padded chairs, and facility staff for setup and teardown at no additional rental fee[[5]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/event-faq/). Weekend reservations incur a standard $100.00 per-floor facility fee for space prep and post-event maintenance[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).

### Secondary Alternative: UC Berkeley Alumni House (Toll Room)

If availability at the David Brower Center is restricted, the **Toll Room at UC Berkeley Alumni House**—located on the campus grounds near Bancroft Way and Dana Street—serves as a qualified secondary venue[[3]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/cal-spirit/alumni-house-rental/). Registered student organizations can reserve the Toll Room for a half-day block (up to 4 hours) at a base rate of $600.00[[9]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Alumni-House-Rental-Rates.pdf). Mandatory operational line items include $120.00 for facility setup and cleanup, $60.00 for on-site event assistants, an $80.00 furniture surcharge, $150.00 for projector and screen rentals, $30.00 for a PA system, and $35.00 for a wireless microphone[[9]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Alumni-House-Rental-Rates.pdf). This yields a baseline facility cost of $1,075.00[[9]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Alumni-House-Rental-Rates.pdf). Although viable, the venue requires separate AV setup coordination compared to the fully integrated technical infrastructure at the David Brower Center[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).

## Catering Strategy and Allergen Management Plan

### Caterer Selection: Gather Catering

For food service, **Gather Catering** is selected as the designated culinary provider[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/). Gather operates directly on-site within the David Brower Center as its primary culinary partner[[1]](https://www.mihiphotobooth.com/post/best-events-venue-2026-berkeley-ca/). This structural integration eliminates off-site transport logistics, ensures food is served immediately upon preparation, and eliminates third-party delivery fees[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/). Gather specializes in organic, farm-to-table Northern California cuisine and maintains extensive experience in executing custom menus for groups with severe dietary restrictions[[2]](https://www.downtownberkeley.com/venues/).

### Universal Allergen Elimination and Menu Engineering

The catering plan must accommodate 30 attendees across four explicit dietary categories:

- **Two Egg Allergies**: Complete elimination of egg whites, egg yolks, lecithin binders, and egg-based emulsifiers[[3]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/cal-spirit/alumni-house-rental/).
- **Two Peanut Allergies**: Strict exclusion of peanuts, peanut oil, peanut flour, and peanut-derived flavorings[[3]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/cal-spirit/alumni-house-rental/).
- **One Combination Allergy (Dairy + Tree Nuts/Peanuts + Shellfish)**: Complete exclusion of all mammalian dairy products (butter, cheese, milk, cream), tree nuts (almonds, walnuts, cashews, pecans), peanuts, and crustacean/mollusk shellfish[[3]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/cal-spirit/alumni-house-rental/).
- **One Vegetarian**: Exclusion of poultry, beef, pork, fish, shellfish, and animal-derived gelatins or broths[[3]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/cal-spirit/alumni-house-rental/).

To minimize operational risk and eliminate cross-contact potential during service, a **Universal Allergen Elimination Strategy** is implemented across the entire catering order[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/). The core menu served to all 30 guests is completely free of peanuts, tree nuts, and shellfish[[3]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/cal-spirit/alumni-house-rental/). Furthermore, all entrees, salads, and desserts are prepared dairy-free and egg-free as a standard baseline, ensuring that cross-contamination risks are systematically removed at the kitchen level[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/).

### Operational Kitchen and Service Protocols

To maintain absolute dietary safety, Gather Catering will enforce standardized cross-contact protocols during prep and execution[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/):

- **Sterilized Prep Areas**: Dairy-free, nut-free, and plant-based dishes will be prepared on dedicated, sanitized stainless steel surfaces prior to non-vegetarian protein assembly[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/).
- **Service Verification**: Plates designated for guests with severe allergies will be marked with discrete, color-coded service markers on the plate rim[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/). Service staff will verbally re-confirm the restriction upon placing the plate (e.g., "Confirming the dairy, nut, and shellfish-free meal")[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/).

### Financial Synthesis and Budget Cap Buffer

The baseline execution cost totals $2,500.10 for a weekend event, landing within ten cents of the primary $2,500.00 target budget[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/). If the banquet is held on a weekday, the $100.00 weekend facility fee is waived, reducing the total expenditure to $2,400.10[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).

Against the user's hard budget cap of $3,000.00, this operational plan maintains an unallocated cash reserve of **$499.90** on weekends ($599.90 on weekdays)[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/). This reserve protects the student group from unexpected operational costs, such as extra AV staff time billed at $60.00 per hour beyond the included three hours, or janitorial overtime billed at $55.00 per hour if the event runs over time[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).

## Regulatory Compliance, Risk Mitigation, and Execution Roadmap

### Sustainability and Environmental Building Mandates

As a LEED Platinum facility, the David Brower Center enforces strict environmental operating guidelines that govern catered events[[1]](https://www.mihiphotobooth.com/post/best-events-venue-2026-berkeley-ca/):

- **Food Waste Mitigation**: The venue monitors post-event waste generation. Unreasonable food waste at the conclusion of an event is subject to an excess food waste fee of up to $300.00[[8]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/goldman-theater/). Gather Catering prevents waste through precise portion control and compostable packaging for leftovers[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/).
- **Prohibited Decorative Materials**: Disposable foamcore signage is strictly banned within the building. Leaving foamcore materials behind triggers a $150.00 disposal fee[[8]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/goldman-theater/). All presentation graphics must be projected via the AV system or mounted on recyclable paper stock[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).

### Contractual Deadlines and Cancellation Liabilities

Venue lease contracts at the David Brower Center enforce specific refund schedules[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/):

- **Cancellations >90 Days Prior**: 100% of the initial booking deposit is fully refunded[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).
- **Cancellations 30–90 Days Prior**: 100% of the booking deposit is retained by the venue[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).
- **Cancellations <30 Days Prior**: 100% of the total contract rental fee is retained[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).

### Operational Execution Sequence

To execute the event smoothly, the student group organizing committee should follow this sequential timeline:

1. **Space Reservation**: Submit an official booking inquiry to `events@browercenter.org` to secure the Tamalpais Room for a 4-hour Quarter Day block[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/). Confirm university registration status to apply the Colleges & Universities rate tier ($590.00 space fee + $425.00 AV package)[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).
2. **Catering Engagement**: Contact Gather Catering (`Jodi Munson: 510-809-0400`) to confirm the allergen-safe menu[[6]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/catering-event-partners/). Provide the final head count (30 guests) and submit the allergen matrix prohibiting peanuts, tree nuts, and shellfish, while confirming the egg-free, dairy-free, and vegetarian plated meals[[3]](https://alumni.berkeley.edu/cal-spirit/alumni-house-rental/).
3. **AV Technical Alignment**: Deliver presentation slides (16:9 widescreen format) to the venue's house AV technician 72 hours prior to the event to verify laptop interface compatibility with the room's projection system[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).
4. **Day-of Event Management**: Arrive 45 minutes prior to the event start time to conduct a walkthrough with the David Brower Center House Manager and Gather Catering lead[[5]](https://browercenter.org/rentals/event-faq/). Confirm slide deck projection, microphone sound levels, table setup, and dietary plate marking before guest arrival[[4]](https://browercenter.org/event-room/tamalpais-room/).
