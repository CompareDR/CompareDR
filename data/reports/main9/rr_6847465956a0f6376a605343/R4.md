# End-of-Year Banquet Plan for ~30 Guests Near Berkeley

Recommended plan: book the **YWCA Berkeley/Oakland Main Lounge** and cater with **Comal Berkeley**’s build-your-own taco bar / burrito bowls for 30. The Main Lounge seats 45 indoors and offers a $200 tech package with a projector/screen or TV; the YWCA allows outside caterers and charges $115/hr on weekend evenings ([YWCA Community Space Rentals](https://www.ywca-berkeley.org/ywca-room-rentals)). ## Venue: YWCA Berkeley/Oakland Main Lounge

Of the Berkeley-area venues that seat 30 and provide or allow a projector, the YWCA Main Lounge is the only one found that pairs a 45-seat indoor room, a projector/screen path, outside-caterer flexibility, and a published hourly rate ([YWCA Community Space Rentals](https://www.ywca-berkeley.org/ywca-room-rentals)). ### How the menu covers the six restricted guests

The six restricted guests collapse into five excluded allergen classes — egg, peanut, tree nut, dairy, shellfish — plus one vegetarian main. The Big-9 are the nine U.S. allergens that account for about 90% of reactions: milk, eggs, shellfish, fish, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, wheat, and sesame ([UC Berkeley Dining — Special Diets](https://dining.berkeley.edu/nutrition/special-diets/)).

| Guest group | Must exclude | Hidden-source watchlist | Coverage on this menu |
|---|---|---|---|
| Egg allergy ×2 | Egg and egg derivatives | Aioli, fresh pasta, batter/breading, baked goods, marshmallows, meringue, some sauces; Comal’s chipotle aioli and flan/shortbread | Base menu egg-free; remove aioli; substitute dessert |
| Peanut allergy ×2 | Peanut and peanut derivatives | Thai peanut sauce, satay, peanut oil, mole/granola/pesto, nut mixes; Comal’s “nut-free” claim conflicts with pecans in its cookie | Written peanut-free confirmation; exclude cookie and mole |
| Dairy + tree nut + shellfish ×1 | Milk, tree nuts, crustaceans + mollusks | Butter, cheese, cream, ghee; pesto, mole, granola, marzipan; shrimp, crab, scallops, clams, oysters, surimi, oyster/shrimp sauce; shared fryers | Binding constraint; order rice without queso fresco, no aioli/cotija on corn, whole beans, no ceviche, dedicated-bakery dessert |
| Vegetarian ×1 | Meat/fish/poultry; confirm egg/dairy | Meat broths, gelatin, lard in refried beans, mole with chicken stock | Vegan main (Tofu Chile Verde/vegetable tacos) + whole black beans + rice/vegetables; covers a vegan interpretation |

Two clarifications matter. First, “nuts” should be read as **both peanut and tree nut** unless the combined-allergy guest confirms otherwise; peanut is a legume, while tree nuts include almond, Brazil nut, cashew, hazelnut, macadamia, pecan, pistachio, walnut, and pine nut ([UC Berkeley Dining — Special Diets](https://dining.berkeley.edu/nutrition/special-diets/)). Second, “shellfish” should exclude both **crustaceans** (crab, crayfish, lobster, prawns, shrimp) and **mollusks** (clams, mussels, oysters, scallops, octopus, snail, squid); fin fish is not automatically excluded for this guest ([Food Allergy Canada](https://foodallergycanada.ca/allergies/crustaceans-and-molluscs/)).

A **single base menu free of all five classes plus a vegan main** is the recommended design because one guest carries three overlapping allergies; this avoids per-guest labeling and cross-contact from separate preparations. Labeled alternatives are a fallback, not the default — if a dish containing dairy or egg is kept for the table, it must be labeled, served only to guests without that restriction, and prepared with dedicated utensils and a separate prep area. Vegetarian means no meat or fish; vegan means no animal products of any kind, including meat, eggs, and dairy ([UC Berkeley Dining — Special Diets](https://dining.berkeley.edu/nutrition/special-diets/)). Default to a vegan main so the vegetarian plate is also safe for the egg- and dairy-allergic guests; confirm whether the vegetarian guest avoids egg and dairy.

### Hidden-ingredient modifications

- **Rice:** order without queso fresco (dairy); confirm oil, not butter.
- **Roasted corn:** remove chipotle aioli (egg) and cotija (dairy); lime/chile only.
- **Black beans:** use whole beans, not refried beans that may contain lard.
- **Chicken:** verify the marinade is egg-, peanut-, dairy-, tree-nut-, and shellfish-free.
- **Dessert:** Comal’s arroz con leche, flan, and Oaxacan chocolate budin contain dairy and egg; Matt’s chocolate-chunk cookie contains pecans. Substitute a fresh fruit platter plus dedicated-bakery egg-free, dairy-free, tree-nut-free cookies or sorbet ([Comal food menu](https://www.comalberkeley.com/menu/food/)).
- **Shellfish:** do not serve ceviche; confirm no surimi or oyster/shrimp sauce in salsas and marinades.
- **Peanut/tree nut:** Comal’s “nut-free” claim conflicts with pecans in its cookie; get written clarification of the claim’s scope and exclude mole and the cookie.

### Cross-contact protocol

Cross-contact is the transfer of allergen proteins from one food to another via shared surfaces, utensils, fryers, or gloves; even tiny amounts can cause reactions ([FARE — Avoiding Cross-Contact](https://www.foodallergy.org/resources/avoiding-cross-contact)). Require in writing:

1. Full ingredient list for every dish, including marinades, salsas, aioli, frying oil, and dessert.
2. Allergen matrix mapping each dish to the Big-9 plus sesame/gluten.
3. Cross-contact statement: dedicated prep surfaces and utensils, separate fryer for chips, glove changes, no shared serving utensils, cleaning of shared grills.
4. Buffet labeling: ingredient cards at each dish; “contains” labels; color-coded cards for the six restricted guests; a pre-plated dedicated plate for the dairy + tree-nut + shellfish guest.
5. Service flow: restricted guests served first or given a pre-plated meal; staff stationed at the buffet.
6. Staff briefing on the six restrictions and the emergency response plan.
7. Post-event check-in with the six guests within 24 hours.

## Allergy safety and emergency protocol

Allergy safety is a pass/fail gate, not a trade-off. The menu above is designed as one base menu free of all five excluded classes plus a vegan main, which is safer than preparing separate labeled plates in a shared kitchen ([FARE — Avoiding Cross-Contact](https://www.foodallergy.org/resources/avoiding-cross-contact)). UC Berkeley Dining’s own disclaimer is the benchmark for what a shared kitchen can promise: “Cross-contact may also occur despite our best efforts,” and its staff are not trained to administer epinephrine and cannot provide or administer it ([UC Berkeley Dining — Allergies](https://dining.berkeley.edu/nutrition/allergies/)).

**Single owner: Safety lead.** Pre-event: collect emergency contacts and allergy action plans from the six restricted guests; confirm each guest carries their own epinephrine auto-injector; send the final restriction list to the caterer and require written ingredient lists/an allergen matrix; print buffet labels. Day-of: the Safety lead carries a printed emergency card; confirm auto-injectors are on guests’ persons; brief the caterer and venue on the restrictions.

If a reaction is suspected: have the guest use their epinephrine auto-injector immediately and call 911 — do not wait. Anaphylaxis is a severe, potentially life-threatening allergic reaction that can impair breathing and must be treated promptly with epinephrine ([UC Berkeley Dining — Special Diets](https://dining.berkeley.edu/nutrition/special-diets/)). Venue or caterer staff may not be trained or permitted to administer epinephrine; the guest’s own auto-injector plus 911 is the response ([FARE Emergency Care Plan](https://www.foodallergy.org/living-food-allergy/food-allergy-essentials/food-allergy-anaphylaxis-emergency-care-plan)).

**Nearest ER:** Alta Bates Summit Medical Center, 2450 Ashby Avenue, Berkeley, CA 94705, 510-204-4444, open 24 hours ([Sutter Health](https://www.sutterhealth.org/find-location/facility/alta-bates-summit-medical-center-alta-bates-campus-1043246519)). For non-emergency follow-up, UC Berkeley UHS Tang Center urgent care, UHS (510) 642-2000, 24/7 advice nurse (510) 643-7197 ([UHS](https://uhs.berkeley.edu/medical/urgent-care/local-emergency-and-urgent-care-centers)).

## Booking timeline and day-of operations

Mid-May through June is commencement season; UC Berkeley’s Spring 2026 Commencement is Saturday, May 16, 2026, so dates before May 16 are somewhat easier ([UC Berkeley Spring Commencement](https://commencement.berkeley.edu/spring/)). ### AV test and contingencies

Test from 5:45–6:15 pm, before doors, and require AV-lead sign-off before guests arrive. Bring an HDMI cable plus a USB-C-to-HDMI adapter; the tech package includes a projector with screen or a TV, speaker, microphone, podium, and camera ([YWCA](https://www.ywca-berkeley.org/ywca-room-rentals)). Set slides to 16:9, route audio through the included speaker, and keep a second laptop, USB drive, and printed handouts as last resorts.

- **Projector failure:** switch to the TV option already included in the $200 tech package. If both fail, rent from All Pro Audiovisual, whose Level 1 unit is 3,500 lumens with free two-way shipping if ordered 7 days ahead; confirm Berkeley delivery ([All Pro Audiovisual](https://allproaudiovisual.com/projectorrentals/)). Campus Berkeley Audio Visual accepts student/RSO requests with a 5-business-day deadline, but off-campus eligibility is unverified ([Berkeley Audio Visual](https://eventservices.berkeley.edu/berkeley-audio-visual-3/)). Last resort: present from a laptop screen.
- **Power outage:** the YWCA page states no backup power. Move to phone-based audio and printed slides, and confirm the venue’s policy in advance.
- **Caterer late or wrong order:** confirm the order in writing 48–72 hours before and store the Comal coordinator’s phone number. Because delivery ends at 6:00 pm, a late delivery cannot be redelivered — switch to pickup or a pre-arranged backup such as grocery platters or a backup caterer (Humbowl or I-House). Pre-arrange the backup; same-day catering is unlikely.
- **Headcount increase:** Comal requires orders at least 24 hours in advance, so a same-day increase is not possible. Order 4 taco-bar kits (covers ~40 guests) instead of 3, or 3 kits plus extra sides; the YWCA Main Lounge seats 45 ([YWCA](https://www.ywca-berkeley.org/ywca-room-rentals)). Confirm the final count with Comal 24+ hours before the event.
