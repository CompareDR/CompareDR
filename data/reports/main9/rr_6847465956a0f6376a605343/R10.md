## The Kinzie Room at the David Brower Center

**Address:** 2150 Allston Way, Berkeley, CA 94704 — one block from the Downtown Berkeley BART station, walking distance from UC Berkeley campus [1].

**Why this room:** The Kinzie Room is sized for a group of 30. Official capacity: banquet seating 56, classroom 31, U-shape 30, theater 75, standing reception 80 [1]. Banquet-style seating at 6 round tables of 5 fits the group comfortably, with room for a projector screen at the front and a food table along the side wall.

**Rate (Colleges & Universities tier, weekday evening):**
- Half Day (up to 8 hrs, 4pm–midnight): $845 [1]
- Quarter Day (up to 4 hrs): $515 [1]

A Half Day window leaves time for setup (5:00 pm), socializing (6:00 pm), food service (6:30 pm), and the slide program (7:30 pm) without rushing.

**AV package: $350** — includes a 50" HDMI monitor (slides display directly from a laptop via HDMI), a PA system, up to 2 wireless handheld mics, and 3 hours of AV staff time [1]. The Kinzie Room has a built-in projection screen on the wall. For a larger projected image, request a portable projector as an add-on (typically +$50–$100 at the Brower Center; confirm with the events team). For 30 people in a U-shape or banquet setup, the 50" monitor is usually sufficient and easier to read in a lit room.

**Furniture included at no charge:** 29 rectangular 72" banquet tables, 15 60" round banquet tables, 225 padded chairs, 2 lecterns, coat racks, whiteboards. The Brower Center does not provide linens — bring or rent [1].

**Optional fees to watch for:**
- Weekend facility fee: +$100/floor [1]
- Security guard (may be required for evening events with alcohol): $700 flat, 5-hr minimum [1]
- Janitorial: $55/hr, 3-hr minimum [1]
- Major contract change fee: $100 per change [1]

For a student banquet with no alcohol, a security guard is unlikely to be required. Confirm with the events director when sending the inquiry.

**Payment & cancellation:** 50% booking deposit at contract signing; remaining 50% one month before the event. 100% of deposit refunded if cancelled more than 90 days out; deposit retained 30–90 days out; 100% of full rental retained if cancelled within 30 days [1].

**Lead time:** Brower Center requires a minimum of 15 business days notice for booking inquiries [1]. For a stress-free booking with menu planning, send the inquiry 6–8 weeks ahead.

## Gather Restaurant (In-House Brower Center Caterer)

**Address:** 2200 Oxford Street, Berkeley, CA 94704 — the ground floor of the David Brower Center [2]. This is not a coincidence: Gather is the Brower Center's in-house catering partner, so the cross-approval and outside-caterer surcharge that would apply to other vendors are waived [3].

**Contact:** Jodi Munson, 510-809-0400, info@gatherrestaurant.com [2][3].

**Service style:** Drop-off (Gather staff deliver, set platters on provided tables; the Brower Center facilities team handles table/chair setup) or full service with on-site staff. Drop-off is sufficient for this banquet and saves on staffing fees [2][3].

**Why Gather is the right call here:** Their catering menu is explicitly labeled for dietary restrictions (V, VG, GF, N) [2]. Several items are naturally free of eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, and shellfish — which means a single menu can safely cover all six restricted guests without parallel meal prep. The Mediterranean-mezze structure below is a deliberate design choice: it removes the cross-contamination risk that comes with running separate "regular" and "allergen" meals, and the 24 unrestricted guests still get a varied, satisfying spread.

**Lead time:** Box lunches require a minimum of 8; for drop-off platter orders, 5–7 business days is standard. For full-service or custom requests, 2–3 weeks is safer [2].

This combination is structured so that every savory item is safe for every restricted guest; the only verification step is the pesto/pistou on the mushroom sandwich and the cookie recipe. With those two clarifications, the entire spread is allergen-safe and substantial enough for 30 diners.

### Dietary Coverage Matrix

| Guest restriction | What they can eat from this menu |
|---|---|
| 2 egg allergies | All savory items (hummus, mushroom sandwich, salads, fruit). Skip cookies unless confirmed egg-free. |
| 2 peanut allergies | All items. None of Gather's standard ingredients contain peanuts. |
| 1 dairy + nuts + shellfish | All savory items. Confirm pesto/pistou is nut-free and dairy-free with Gather. No cheese anywhere on the menu. |
| 1 vegetarian | All items — fully plant-based menu. |
| 24 unrestricted | Full spread. The variety of proteins (chickpea hummus, tofu, mushrooms) and the substantial portions from 4 platters per category cover omnivores well, even without meat. |

The matrix shows a single fully plant-based menu covers every restriction without any parallel "allergen" meal prep. That is the core reason the Mediterranean mezze design beats a traditional meat-and-dairy banquet at this headcount.

### Menu Trade-offs

There is no hot entrée, no animal protein, and no traditional banquet dessert. This is a deliberate trade: it costs less, accommodates all six restricted guests without separate meals, and avoids the cross-contamination risk of running a meat/dairy menu alongside allergen-safe alternatives. If the group strongly expects a hot protein main, the easiest swap is to add a Roasted Chicken entrée platter ($120, serves 10) — but this brings back eggs (in dressings) and dairy, requiring separate meals for the 6 restricted guests and re-introducing the cross-contamination problem the mezze structure was designed to avoid.

### Plates, Utensils, Napkins

Gather's drop-off catering includes basic disposables. For 30 people, budget $30–$50 for an extra supply of plates, cutlery, napkins, and serving utensils (compostable at a hardware store or restaurant supply). The Brower Center's furniture is included; linens must be rented separately if desired [1].

## Backup Plan

If the Kinzie Room is unavailable on the chosen date, or if a projector (not just a 50" monitor) is a hard requirement:

**Alternative venue: Giggster "Presentation, Summit, and Forum Space" — Downtown Berkeley**

This space holds up to 85 (well over 30), rents at $125/hour with a 4-hour minimum, and comes with a built-in drop-down projector screen and projector, podium mic, AV, and refrigerated storage for catering [6]. A 4-hour evening rental runs ~$500 and external catering is allowed, leaving ~$1,800 for food at $60/person across 30 guests.

**Alternative caterer (if Gather is unavailable): Freehouse Berkeley**

Located at 2700 Bancroft Way, a 10-minute walk from the Brower Center and most other downtown venues [7]. Freehouse's buffet menu includes explicit allergy-aware options: Hummus with Crudité ($60/10 ppl), Beyond Burger Platter ($100/10 ppl, vegetarian and free of eggs/dairy/nuts/shellfish), Organic Vegan & Gluten Free Salad ($70/30 ppl), and Crispy Brussels Sprouts ($63/10 ppl) [7]. A 20% gratuity is added to all catering events [7]. Freehouse is less naturally allergen-friendly than Gather's menu (most proteins are cheese- or egg-based), so the menu design requires more care.

**Other vetted Brower Center catering partners** (all on the approved list, no outside-caterer surcharge) [3]: The Mixing Bowl Catering, ACT Catering, Hugh Groman Group, La Bonne Catering, The Table Catering, Aurora Catering. Any of these can replicate the mezze structure; ask for their allergen-free or vegan platters when requesting quotes.

## Pre-Booking Confirmations

Three details will determine whether this plan lands cleanly. Send these questions in writing when reaching out to both vendors.

**To Brower Center (events@browercenter.org):**
1. Is the Kinzie Room available on the chosen date in the 4pm–midnight window?
2. Is a security guard required for a 30-person student-group evening event with no alcohol?
3. Can a portable projector be added to the AV package, and at what cost?
4. Are linens available to rent, or should we source them separately?

**To Gather (Jodi Munson, 510-809-0400):**
1. Is the basil pistou on the Roasted Mushroom Sandwich confirmed nut-free and dairy-free? (The Provençal recipe is traditionally both, but Gather's kitchen may source it differently.)
2. Is the Salted Chocolate Cookies recipe egg-free and dairy-free? If not, can a fruit-based or other allergen-free dessert be substituted at the same price?
3. Can the order be drop-off with setup at 5:30 pm, with platters replenished at 7:00 pm if needed?
4. Does the 18% gratuity apply to drop-off orders, or only to full-service?

If Gather cannot confirm the pesto/pistou and cookie questions satisfactorily, the fallback is to swap both items: replace the second mushroom sandwich platter with another Harvest Bowl (+$90) and replace the cookies with a second Farm Fresh Fruit platter (+$70). The menu stays at the same allergen coverage and gains $20 in headroom.
